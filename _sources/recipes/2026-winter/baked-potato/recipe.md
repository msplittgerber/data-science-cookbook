# Template
This dish really comes in handy when you have other things to do. You can clean the house or do other chores while
it just does it's thing.

![A super cool image of a baked sweet potato](sweetpotato.png)

**Provided by:** Erik K.

## Stats
- Cooking Time: Around 40-55 minutes (depending on the size of the potato)
- Servings: Just one...

## Ingredients
- One Sweet potato (roughly 400-650g)
- A dash of olive oil
- Salt
- A protein of your choice (Tofu, Chicken, Ground Beef)
- 125g cheese
- Optional: Vegetables as a side (broccoli, carrots)

## Instructions
- Peel the sweet potato
- Use a fork to make a few holes in the sweet potato
- Apply a dash of olive oil and rub it on the sweet potato
- Put it in the airfryer at 180 °C for 35-45 minutes (depending on the size of the potato)
- Prepare your protein (frying it in a pan or use left overs from the day before)
- Cut the sweet potato in half and use a fork to mush up the inner part
- Put your protein inside the sweet potato and cover it with cheese
- Airfry for 8-10 more minutes
- Enjoy
