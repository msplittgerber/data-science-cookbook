# Bánh Xèo (Vietnamese Sizzling Crepes)
Bánh Xèo is a crispy, savory Vietnamese crepe stuffed with pork, prawns, and bean sprouts. The name literally translates to "sizzling cake," named after the loud sizzling sound the rice batter makes when poured into a hot skillet.

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 45 minutes
- Servings: 4 people

## Ingredients
### For the Batter:
- 200g      Rice flour
- 1/2 tsp   Turmeric powder
- 250ml     Water (or coconut milk)
- 100ml     Carbonated water (or beer, for extra crispiness)
- 1/2 tsp   Salt
- 3 stalks  Green onions (finely chopped)

### For the Filling:
- 200g      Pork belly (thinly sliced)
- 200g      Shrimp (peeled and deveined)
- 100g      Bean sprouts
- 1 piece   Mung bean (optional, cooked until soft)
- Cooking oil

### For Serving:
- Mustard greens, lettuce leaves, mint, perilla, and sweet-and-sour fish sauce (nước chấm)

## Instructions
- **Step 1:** In a large bowl, whisk together the rice flour, turmeric powder, and salt. Pour in the water, carbonated water (or beer), and chopped green onions. Whisk until smooth and let the batter rest for 30 minutes.
- **Step 2:** Heat a small amount of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add a few slices of pork and shrimp, cooking them until lightly seared.
- **Step 3:** Stir the batter well. Pour a ladleful into the skillet, quickly swirling it to create a thin, even layer covering the bottom. It should make a loud sizzling sound.
- **Step 4:** Scatter a small handful of bean sprouts (and cooked mung beans if using) over one half of the crepe.
- **Step 5:** Cover the skillet with a lid and cook for 2-3 minutes until the sprouts wilt slightly.
- **Step 6:** Remove the lid, lower the heat to medium, and continue cooking for another 2-3 minutes until the edges turn golden brown and crispy.
- **Step 7:** Fold the crepe in half over the filling and transfer it to a serving plate. Repeat the process with the remaining batter and filling.
- **Step 8:** Serve hot wrapped in lettuce leaves and fresh herbs, dipped in sweet-and-sour fish sauce.
