# Fudgy Chocolate Brownies

This is a recipe for Brownies that have a slightly crispy top and a soft Center. I love this recipe because it is easy to make and very tasty :)

**Provided by:** amalramia

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 40 minutes (15 minutes preparation + 25 minutes baking)
- Servings: 9 brownies

## Ingredients
- 150 g unsalted butter
- 180 g dark chocolate
- 150 g sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 90 g all-purpose flour
- 25 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. Line a baking pan with parchment paper.
3. Melt the butter and dark chocolate together over low heat until smooth. Let the mixture cool.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs, sugar, and vanilla extract together.
5. Add the melted chocolate mixture and stir until combined.
6. Add the flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Gently mix until no dry flour remains.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan.
8. Bake for 25 minutes. A toothpick inserted into the center should come out with a few moist Crumbs (otherwise the Brownie gets too dry).
9. Let the brownies cool before cutting them into squares.

## Tips
- For extra fudgy brownies, avoid overbaking them. The center should still be slightly soft.
- Let the brownies cool completely before cutting them into squares.
- For a richer chocolate flavor, add some chocolate chips to the batter.
- Store the brownies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
