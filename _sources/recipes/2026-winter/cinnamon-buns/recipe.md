# Cinnamon buns
This is a recipe for vegan cinnamon buns. I especially like to make them in autumn and winter and they remind me of Sweden.

![Cinnamon buns](image.jpg)

**Provided by:** Finja ([original recipe](https://veggie-einhorn.de/saftige-vegane-zimtschnecken/))

## Stats
- Total Time: 1h 55min
- Servings: 12 cinnamon buns

## Ingredients
Yeast Dough

- 500 g flour
- 1 packet dry yeast or ½ cube fresh yeast
- 100 g sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- 50 g plant-based margarine
- 250 g plant-based milk = 250 ml (e.g. soy milk, oat milk…)

Cinnamon Filling

- 60 g plant-based margarine
- 60 g sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon

Icing

- 100 g powdered sugar
- 1 tbsp plant-based milk


## Instructions
Making the Yeast Dough

- In a bowl, mix:
  500 g flour
  1 packet dry yeast
  100 g sugar
  ½ tsp salt
- Mix the dry ingredients well.
- Add:
  250 g plant-based milk (= 250 ml, e.g. soy milk, oat milk, almond milk)
  50 g vegan butter (Alsan margarine)
  The vegan butter must be soft (room temperature).
  (If you are using fresh yeast: add ½ cube now.)
- Knead the dough for 10 minutes.
  Use the dough hooks of a hand mixer or a stand mixer.
  Do not add more liquid. It takes about 4 minutes for everything to come together nicely.
  Knead for a total of 10 minutes, until the dough is smooth and no longer sticky.
- Cover the bowl with the dough with a damp towel or plastic wrap.
  Let it rise for one hour.
  (Longer is fine too.)

Preparing the Filling

- Melt 60 g vegan butter (Alsan margarine).
  (In a small pot or very briefly in the microwave.)
  Not too hot. Just warm enough that it barely becomes liquid.
- Add to the melted margarine:
  60 g sugar
  2 tsp cinnamon
  Stir well.
- Then let the mixture cool so it firms up.
  If the filling is too warm, it is too runny and easily leaks out of the cinnamon rolls.
  That is why it needs to cool.
  (Or spread the dough with soft margarine and then sprinkle with a dry sugar-cinnamon mixture.)

Rolling Out and Filling the Dough

- Grease a baking dish (approx. 25 x 35 cm).
- Dust the work surface and rolling pin with a little flour.
- Roll out the yeast dough into a rectangle.
  Approx. 30 x 42 cm
  The exact measurements don't matter that much.
  The main thing is that it is a rectangle.
  (Not an oval.)
- Spread the filling over the dough and smooth it out evenly.
  (Or spread the dough with soft margarine and then sprinkle with a dry sugar-cinnamon mixture.)
- Roll up the dough from the long side.
  (You will then have a roll approx. 42 cm long.)
- Cut the roll into slices.
  Use a sharp knife.
  12 slices.
  Approx. 3.5 cm thick.
  (Or place a thread around the roll and pull it tight to cut through. This works with dental floss, for example. I always use a sharp knife though, as it's faster.)
- Place the slices in the greased baking dish.
  (e.g. 3 rows of 4 cinnamon rolls each)
- Cover the baking dish with a damp towel.
  Let the cinnamon rolls rise again for 30 minutes.

Baking the Cinnamon Rolls

- Preheat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F) (conventional/top and bottom heat).
- Spray or brush the cinnamon rolls in the baking dish with a little water.
  (Not too much.)
  Then put them into the oven immediately (middle rack).
- Bake for 25 minutes at 180 °C (conventional/top and bottom heat).
  The cinnamon rolls should be nicely golden brown.
- Let cool slightly.

Icing

- Sift 100 g powdered sugar (icing sugar) for the icing.
- Stir smooth with 1 tbsp plant-based milk. (Not too much liquid, otherwise the icing will be too thin.)
- Drizzle the icing over the lukewarm cinnamon rolls.
