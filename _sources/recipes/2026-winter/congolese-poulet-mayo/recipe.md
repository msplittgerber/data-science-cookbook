# Congolese Poulet Mayo

Poulet mayo is a marinated, oven-roasted chicken from the Democratic Republic of the Congo. It is special to me because it reminds me of all the celebrations I had around this dish when I was in my country: dancing with friends and family to rumba music. In all our celebrations, this dish is essential. It is easy and quick to make, tastes great, and can be eaten with plantains, potato fries or kwanga, with a Congolese drink on the side. It is the lively atmosphere that turns a simple meal into an experience.

## Stats
- Cooking Time: about 1 h 30 min of preparation and cooking, plus at least 2 hours of marinating (the time depends on the size of the chicken and the number of people)
- Servings: 4 (one medium chicken, 1.5-2 kg)


## Ingredients

**Main ingredients**
- 1 whole chicken (1.5-2 kg)
- 5 tablespoons mayonnaise (75 g)
- 1 tablespoon mustard (15 g)
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (30 ml)
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice (15 ml)

**Seasonings and spices**
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 medium onion (about 100 g), chopped
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon chicken bouillon powder
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
- 1/2-1 teaspoon chilli or pili-pili (optional)

**Optional sides**
- 1 kg potatoes for fries
- 3 ripe plantains
- Extra mayonnaise or pili-pili sauce for serving

## Instructions
1. **Prepare the chicken:** Clean the chicken and cut it into pieces, or cut one whole chicken open.
2. **Make the sauce:** In a bowl, mix the mayonnaise, mustard, oil, lemon juice, crushed garlic, chopped onion, paprika, chicken bouillon powder, black pepper, salt, and chilli if you like it spicy.
3. **Add the sauce:** Put the chicken in a large bowl. Pour the sauce over it and rub it all over the chicken.
4. **Let it rest:** Cover the bowl and put it in the fridge for at least 2 hours. Overnight gives more flavour.
5. **Cook the chicken:** Preheat the oven to 200°C. Put the chicken in an oven dish and cook for 45-70 minutes, depending on its size. Turn the pieces halfway through.
6. **Make it golden:** When the chicken is almost cooked, turn on the oven grill for 3-5 minutes to brown the outside. Watch carefully so it does not burn.
7. **Check and serve:** Make sure the thickest part of the chicken reaches 74°C inside. Serve hot with fries, fried plantain and pili-pili sauce.


## Tips and tricks
- Serve it the Congolese way: with fried plantain, potato fries or kwanga, and a Congolese drink on the side.
- Marinate overnight if you can, because the flavour is much better.
- Adjust the pili-pili(chili) to your taste, or serve it on the side so everyone can choose their own heat.
- Best enjoyed with family and friends, and rumba music!
