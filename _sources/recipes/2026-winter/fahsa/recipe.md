## Fahsa – Yemeni Recipe

> **Prep time:** Approx. 2 to 4 hours (depending on how long the meat takes to become fully tender).
> **Yield:** Approx. 500 g of meat

### Ingredients

* **500 g beef**, preferably veal
* **2 tbsp Saman** (Yemeni clarified butter) or oil (plus 1 extra tbsp for the final step)
* **1 medium onion**, finely chopped (or green garlic stalks/scapes)
* **3 garlic cloves**, crushed
* **½ bell pepper**, chopped (traditionally, very spicy chili is used)
* **500 g strained tomatoes** (tomato sauce, not tomato paste)
* **1 tbsp cumin**
* **1 tsp ground coriander**
* **1 tsp black pepper**
* **1 tsp paprika powder**
* **1 tsp salt**
* **Hot water** (as needed to cover the meat)

#### For the Final Step:
* **½ onion**, chopped
* **¼ bell pepper**, chopped
* **1 tbsp Saman** or oil

#### Optional (Helbah / Fenugreek Foam):
* **1 tbsp fenugreek seeds** (soaked in water for about 3 hours, then drained)
* *Optional:* A little juice from fresh radish leaves

---

### Instructions

1. **Sauté the aromatics:** Heat the Saman or oil in a pot until hot. Add the onion (or green garlic) and bell pepper, and sauté for about 2 minutes while stirring.
2. **Add meat and tomatoes:** Add the meat and stir well. Then, mix in the strained tomatoes.
3. **Season and simmer:** Add all the spices and combine thoroughly. Before the liquid reduces too much, pour in enough hot water so that the meat is completely submerged.
4. **Cook until tender:** Simmer over medium heat until the meat is completely soft and tender (about 2 to 4 hours). Stir approximately every 15 minutes and add more hot water as needed to ensure the meat remains covered in liquid at all times.

#### The Final Step
1. **Prepare the base:** In a second pot, heat 1 tbsp of Saman or oil. Add the remaining half-onion and ¼ bell pepper, and sauté until golden.
2. **Shred and combine:** Remove the cooked meat from the first pot and shred it into small pieces. Transfer the shredded meat along with a ladleful of its cooking broth into the second pot.
3. **Mix:** Stir everything well until a uniform consistency is reached.

#### Serving with Optional Helbah
1. Whip the drained, soaked fenugreek seeds vigorously with a whisk or hand mixer until the mixture becomes light, fluffy, and almost white in color. (You may mix in a little radish leaf juice if desired).
2. Spoon the Helbah directly onto the Fahsa right before serving.
