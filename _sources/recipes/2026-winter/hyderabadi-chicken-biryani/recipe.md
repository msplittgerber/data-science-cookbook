# Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani

Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani is a popular Indian rice dish made with aromatic basmati rice, marinated chicken, fresh herbs, and traditional spices. The combination of fragrant rice and spicy chicken makes it a favourite for family meals and special occasions.

**Provided by:** Mohameed Humayun Farhat

## Stats
- Preparation Time: 30 minutes
- Cooking Time: 60 minutes
- Total Time: 90 minutes
- Servings: 4

## Ingredients

### For the chicken marinade
- 750 g chicken, preferably bone-in
- 200 g plain yogurt
- 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 2 teaspoons red chilli powder, adjusted to taste
- 2 teaspoons biryani masala
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- Salt to taste
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- A handful of fresh mint leaves
- A handful of fresh coriander leaves

### For the rice
- 500 g basmati rice
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 green cardamom pods
- 4 cloves
- 1 cinnamon stick
- Salt to taste
- Water for boiling

### Additional ingredients
- 3 medium onions, thinly sliced
- 3 tablespoons cooking oil
- 2 tablespoons ghee
- A pinch of saffron soaked in 2 tablespoons warm milk (optional)
- Extra mint and coriander leaves for layering

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the rice:** Rinse the basmati rice several times until the water runs mostly clear. Soak it for 20–30 minutes, then drain.

2. **Marinate the chicken:** In a large bowl, combine the chicken, yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, chilli powder, biryani masala, garam masala, lemon juice, salt, mint, and coriander. Mix well and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

3. **Fry the onions:** Heat the oil in a large, heavy-bottomed pot. Fry the sliced onions until golden brown. Remove some onions for layering later.

4. **Cook the chicken:** Add the marinated chicken to the pot with the remaining onions. Cook over medium heat for approximately 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally.

5. **Parboil the rice:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add the bay leaves, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and salt. Add the drained rice and cook until it is approximately 70% cooked. Drain immediately.

6. **Layer the biryani:** Spread the partially cooked rice over the chicken. Add the reserved fried onions, mint, coriander, ghee, and saffron milk if using.

7. **Cook on low heat:** Cover the pot tightly and cook over low heat for approximately 25–35 minutes, until the chicken is fully cooked and the rice is tender. If necessary, use a heat diffuser to prevent the bottom from burning.

8. **Rest and serve:** Turn off the heat and let the biryani rest for 10 minutes. Gently mix some of the rice and chicken before serving.

## Tips and Tricks

- Use good-quality basmati rice for a fragrant, fluffy result.
- Marinating the chicken for several hours can improve its flavour.
- Avoid overcooking the rice during the parboiling stage.
- Cook on low heat during the final stage to prevent burning.
- Check that the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 74°C before serving.
- Serve with raita, sliced onions, or a fresh salad.


## Personal Note

I enjoy Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani because of its rich aroma and combination of spices. The key to a good biryani is preparing the rice carefully and cooking the chicken with enough time for the flavours to develop.
