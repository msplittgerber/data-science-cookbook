# Kısır (Turkish Bulgur Salad)

Kısır is a traditional Turkish salad made with fine bulgur,
fresh vegetables, herbs, olive oil, and lemon juice.

It is a popular dish in Turkish cuisine, especially at family
gatherings. I chose this recipe because it is simple to prepare
and represents traditional Turkish home cooking.

**Provided by:** Sefa

## Stats

- Cooking Time: 30 minutes
- Servings: 4

## Ingredients

- 200 g fine bulgur
- 250 ml hot water
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon red pepper paste
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon pomegranate molasses
- 3 spring onions
- 1 medium tomato
- 1 cucumber
- 1 handful fresh parsley
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)

## Instructions

1. Place the fine bulgur in a large bowl and pour 250 ml of hot water over it. Cover the bowl and let it rest for 10-15 minutes until the bulgur absorbs the water.

2. Add the tomato paste, red pepper paste, and olive oil to the softened bulgur. Mix thoroughly until the bulgur has an even reddish color.

3. Finely chop the spring onions, tomato, cucumber, and fresh parsley.

4. Add the chopped vegetables and parsley to the bulgur mixture.

5. Pour in the lemon juice and pomegranate molasses. Add salt, ground cumin, and red pepper flakes if desired.

6. Mix all the ingredients gently until well combined. Taste and adjust the seasoning if necessary.

7. Let the salad rest for 5 minutes to allow the flavors to blend.

8. Serve at room temperature or slightly chilled. You can also serve it with fresh lettuce leaves and lemon wedges.
