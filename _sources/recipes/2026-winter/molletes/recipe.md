# Mexican Molletes  

Molletes are a traditional Mexican dish. Normally eaten for breakfast or dinner. Original made from leftovers, today are now freshly made. Several versions exist, with mushrooms, sausages or ham. Today we are making the basic version. They are made out of bread, beans, cheese and a sauce composed of fresh tomato, onion, jalapeño chili and coriander named *Pico de Gallo*
Out of the Jalapeños, which are not easy to find but easy to substitute for other chili, the ingredients are worldwide available. Being an easy homesick remedy. If you do not eat chili, you can skip it. The tomato, coriander and onion freshness will be enough.

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 30 min
- Servings: 6
## Ingredients
### Beans
- 1 Can of Beans (Kidney or Black) 
- 1/4 Medium Onion
- 1 Garlic Clove
- 20 to 50 ml Milk
- 2 Tbsp Butter
### Sauce
- 2 Tomatoes
- 1/2 Medium Onion 
- 5 g Coriander
- 0 to 2 Jalapeños, depends on how spicy the Chilies are and how much you eat.
- 1/2 Lime or Lemon, Lime is prefered. 
- Salt and Pepper to taste.
### Main Dish
- 3 Bread-rolls (Brötchen)
- 100 g Cheese, any melting cheese would make it, e.g. Gouda or Pizza mix. 
## Tools and Kitchen Aids
- Small Pot (Stainless Steel)
- Sieve
- Potato Masher or Blender
- Oven
- Bread Knife
- Cutting Board
- Bowl
- Metal Table Spoons
## Instructions
### Beans
- Open the beans' can, put them in the sieve and rinse them under the faucet with cold water until the water is crystal clear. Let them drain.
- Cut the onion and garlic clove into small squares/pieces.
- Put the pot in medium heat and wait until it is warm, then add the butter; do not let it burn.
- Once the butter is melted, add the onion. Wait until it begins to get brown. Then add the garlic.
- Once the garlic is soft and begins to get brown add the beans and 20 ml of milk.
- Smash the beans with the potato masher until no whole beans remain. If the mixture is too dry, add more milk. It must not be a liquid, but a paste that you can stir.
- ***Stir and Stir*** This is the critical step. You need to continuously, not with effort, but continuously stir the mix for approx 7 min. Otherwise the beans will stick to the pot's bottom and will get burned. It is suggested to use a metal table spoon. After 7 minutes, turn off the heat and let the beans rest. Stir from time to time. Don't let the beans get stuck into the pot.
### Pico de Gallo
- Let's use time wisely and preheat the oven to 175 °C
- Cut in small pieces the onion, the tomato and the Jalapeño or chili if you are putting some, and the coriander too. Mix everything with the juice of the lime in the bowl. Put salt and pepper to taste. Give a final mix.
### Main Dish 
- Cut the bread into half and remove part of the internal bread crumbs. 
- To each half, add between 2 and 3 tablespoons of beans. Spread them through the bread. 
- Put the cheese on top of the bread and place them on a baking tray. Bake them between 5 and 8 min. Take them out once the cheese has melted or begins to get brown.
- Serve them on a plate and add the pico de gallo just before you eat them.  

### ¡Buen Provecho!, Enjoy!, Guten Appetit!
## Hints and Tricks
- Do not eat them immediately after they are out of the oven. Even if the cheese looks like it has colded down. Wait a couple of minutes, the beans may burn your mouth.
- If you have, add guacamole to them. See the image for a reference.
