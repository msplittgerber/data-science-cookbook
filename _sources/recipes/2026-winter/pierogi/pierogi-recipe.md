# Homemade Polish Pierogi with Potato and Cheese

Polish pierogi are dumplings filled with mashed potatoes and melted Cheddar cheese, wrapped in a sour cream dough. They are traditional Eastern European comfort food and are perfect for sharing with family and friends. For me, they are my childhood food, which we eat on occasions store-bought, and once a year at Christmas, we make them fresh.

## Stats
- Cooking Time: Approximately 1 hour 15 minutes
- Servings: 8
- Cuisine: Polish
- Difficulty: Medium

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 4 ½ cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 cups sour cream
- 2 large eggs
- 1 egg yolk
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

### Potato and Cheese Filling
- 8 baking potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese
- 2 tablespoons processed cheese sauce
- 1 pinch onion salt, or to taste
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the ingredients.** Gather all ingredients and prepare a clean, lightly floured work surface.

2. **Make the dough.** In a large bowl, combine the flour and salt. In a separate bowl, whisk together the sour cream, eggs, egg yolk, melted butter, and vegetable oil until smooth. Add the wet mixture to the flour and stir until a dough forms. Cover with a clean towel and let rest at room temperature for 15–20 minutes.

3. **Cook the potatoes.** Place the peeled, cubed potatoes in a large pot and cover with water. Bring to a boil and cook for about 15 minutes, or until fork-tender. Drain well.

4. **Prepare the filling.** While the potatoes are still hot, mash them with the shredded Cheddar cheese and processed cheese sauce. Season with onion salt, salt, and pepper to taste. Set aside to cool.

5. **Roll out the dough.** Divide the dough into two equal portions. On a lightly floured surface, roll out one portion at a time to approximately 1/4 inch thick.

6. **Cut the circles.** Use a round cookie cutter, pierogi cutter, or drinking glass to cut circles about 3–4 inches in diameter. Gather and reroll any leftover dough.

7. **Fill the pierogi.** Lightly moisten the edges of each dough circle with water. Place a spoonful of the cooled potato and cheese filling in the center, taking care not to overfill.

8. **Seal the dumplings.** Fold each circle in half to form a semicircle. Press the edges firmly together with your fingers or a fork to seal the filling inside.

9. **Freeze or cook.** Arrange the pierogi on a baking sheet in a single layer and freeze until solid. Transfer them to freezer-safe bags or containers for storage, or cook them immediately.

10. **Boil the pierogi.** Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add the pierogi in batches and cook for approximately 5 minutes, until they float to the surface and are heated through. Avoid overcooking to keep the dough tender.

11. **Serve and enjoy.** Remove the pierogi with a slotted spoon and serve hot. For extra flavor, toss them in melted butter or pan-fry them until golden. Sour cream and sautéed onions make delicious accompaniments.

**Tip:** Let the filling cool before assembling the pierogi, and make sure each dumpling is sealed well to prevent it from opening during cooking.
