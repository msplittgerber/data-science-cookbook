# Qutab (Azerbaijani Stuffed Flatbread)
Qutab is a thin, half-moon shaped flatbread from Azerbaijan, filled with fresh herbs and cooked in a dry pan. It is quick, vegetarian and tastes like home to me.


**Provided by:** Ali

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 50 minutes (including 20 minutes of resting the dough)
- Servings: 4 (about 8 qutabs)

## Ingredients
**Dough**
- 300 g flour
- 150 ml lukewarm water
- 1/2 tsp salt

**Filling**
- 200 g spinach
- 100 g fresh herbs (dill, cilantro, spring onions)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Black pepper

**To finish**
- 1 tbsp melted butter
- Yogurt and sumac for serving

## Instructions
- Knead flour, water and salt into a smooth, soft dough. Cover it and let it rest for 20 minutes.
- Chop the spinach and herbs finely, squeeze out the excess water, then mix with salt and pepper.
- Divide the dough into 8 pieces and roll each one out very thin (about 20 cm circles).
- Put some filling on one half of each circle, fold over and press the edges together to seal.
- Cook in a hot, dry pan for 2–3 minutes per side until golden spots appear.
- Brush with melted butter and serve hot with yogurt and sumac.

## Tips
- Squeeze the greens well, otherwise the dough gets soggy.
- Roll the dough as thin as possible, it should be almost see-through.
