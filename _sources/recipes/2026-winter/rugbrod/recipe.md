# Rugbrød

Rugbrød is a traditional Danish rye bread made with whole grains and seeds. It is special to me because I spent a lot of time experimenting to perfect my recipe.

![Homemade Rugbrød](image.jpg)

**Provided by:** Berkay

## Stats

- Cooking Time: 60 minutes
- Fermentation Time: 16 hours (refrigerated)
- Resting Time: 2 hours before baking
- Cooling Time: 4 hours
- Final Resting Time: 24 hours
- Yield: 1 loaf

## Ingredients

- 500 g rye flour
- 100 g rolled oats
- 75 g sunflower seeds
- 50 g pumpkin seeds
- 40 g flaxseeds
- 600 ml lukewarm water
- 35 g dried sourdough mix
- 30 g molasses
- 15 g apple cider vinegar
- 10 g salt

## Instructions

1. Place the flaxseeds and rolled oats in a bowl. Add 200 ml of hot water and let them soak for 30 minutes.

2. In a separate bowl, mix the rye flour, sunflower seeds, pumpkin seeds, and salt. This is your dry mixture.

3. In another bowl, whisk together 400 ml of lukewarm water, dried sourdough mix, molasses, and apple cider vinegar. This is your wet mixture.

4. Add the soaked flaxseeds and oats to the dry mixture and stir. Then add the wet mixture. Mix with a wooden spatula until you have a thick, even dough.

5. Transfer the dough to a loaf pan using a spatula. Press it down and smooth the surface, making sure there are no air pockets.

6. Cover the loaf pan with plastic wrap and leave it in the refrigerator for 16 hours to ferment slowly.

7. Remove the dough from the refrigerator and let it rest at room temperature for 2 hours.

8. Preheat the oven to 200°C using conventional heat (top and bottom). Place a small oven-safe container of boiling water inside the oven for the first 25 minutes of baking. Brush the top of the dough with diluted molasses and sprinkle with sunflower seeds or other seeds. Make about 10 small holes in the dough using a toothpick to help prevent cracking.

9. Place the loaf pan in the oven and bake for 1 hour.

10. Remove the bread from the oven and leave it in the pan for 5 minutes. Then remove it from the pan and place it on a wire rack to cool for 4 hours.

11. Wrap the bread in a clean towel and leave it at room temperature for 24 hours before slicing.
