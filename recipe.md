# Üç Bacı Dolması (Stuffed Eggplant, Tomato, and Pepper)

Üç Bacı Dolması is a traditional Azerbaijani dish featuring summer vegetables—eggplants, tomatoes, and bell peppers—stuffed with a savory minced meat filling.

---

##  Prep & Cooking Time
* Preparation Time: 30 minutes
* Cooking Time: 45 minutes
* Servings: 4 persons

---

##  Ingredients

### Main Vegetables ("The Three Sisters")
* 4 medium eggplants (aubergines)
* 4 medium tomatoes
* 4 medium green bell peppers

### Filling
* 500g minced lamb or beef (or a mixture)
* 2 medium onions, finely chopped
* 100g butter (or clarified butter)
* 1/2 cup fresh basil and cilantro (coriander), finely chopped
* Salt and black pepper to taste
* A pinch of turmeric (optional)

---

##  Instructions

### Step 1: Preparing the Vegetables
1. Eggplants: Slit each eggplant lengthwise down one side. Boil them in salted water for 3–5 minutes to soften and remove bitterness. Drain and gently press out excess water.
2. Peppers: Slice off the top stem caps (keep them as lids) and remove the seeds inside.
3. Tomatoes: Slice off the top caps (keep them as lids) and scoop out the inner pulp. Save the pulp for cooking.

### Step 2: Preparing the Filling
1. In a skillet, sauté the chopped onions with butter until soft and golden.
2. Add the minced meat and cook over medium heat until browned.
3. Season with salt, black pepper, turmeric, and chopped herbs. Mix well and remove from heat.

### Step 3: Stuffing and Assembly
1. Fill each eggplant, pepper, and tomato generously with the cooked meat mixture.
2. Cover the peppers and tomatoes with their reserved top caps.
3. Arrange the stuffed vegetables tightly together in a deep pot.
4. Pour the reserved tomato pulp into the bottom of the pot. Do not add extra water, as the vegetables will release their own juices.

### Step 4: Cooking
1. Cover the pot with a lid and cook over low heat for 40 to 45 minutes, until the vegetables are soft and tender.
2. Serve hot with garlic yogurt (qatıq) on the side.

---

> Tip: Enjoy with freshly baked bread!
