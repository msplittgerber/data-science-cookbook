# Chicken Dum Biriyani

Chicken Dum Biryani is made with spicy marinated chicken, fragrant basmati rice, fried onions and fresh herbs. It is special to me because it is full of flavour and reminds me of enjoying a delicious meal with family.

**Provided by:** Lalitha Gadi.

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 45–55 minutes
- Servings: 4 People.

## Ingredients

### For the chicken marination
-  750 g chicken pieces
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
- 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon Garam masala
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- 1 teaspoon biryani masala
- 1/2 tablespoon pepper powder
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 2 green chillies, chopped
- 2 tablespoons oil
- Salt to taste
- Fresh mint and coriander leaves
- Fried onions

### For the rice
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 2–3 green cardamom pods
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 3 cloves
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 teaspoon shajeera
- 2 tablespoons oil or ghee
- 3–4 cups water
- Salt to taste
- Fried onions, mint and coriander leaves for topping
- Saffron soaked in warm milk (optional)

## Instructions
1. Wash the basmati rice and soak it for 20–30 minutes. Drain before cooking.
2. Mix the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, turmeric, garam masala, coriander powder, biryani masala, pepper, lemon juice, green chillies, salt, mint, coriander and some fried onions. Marinate in the refrigerator for at least one hour.
3. Heat oil in a heavy pot and fry the onions until golden brown. Keep some aside for topping.
4. Boil water in another pot with cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, bay leaves, shajeera and salt.
5. Add the soaked rice and cook until it is about 70% done. Drain carefully.
6. Place the marinated chicken in the heavy pot and cook for about 8–10 minutes.
7. Spread the partly cooked rice over the chicken. Add fried onions, mint, coriander and saffron milk if desired.
8. Cover the pot tightly and cook on very low heat until the rice is tender and the chicken is fully cooked. Check that the chicken reaches 74°C (165°F).
9. Turn off the heat and leave the pot covered for 10 minutes.
10. Gently mix and serve hot with Raita, gravy or salad.

## Tips
- Let the biryani rest for 10 minutes after cooking so the flavours settle before serving.
- Marinating the chicken for longer helps the spices soak in.
- Fried onions give the biryani a nice taste and aroma.
- Keep the heat low during dum cooking so the rice and chicken do not burn at the bottom.
- Be gentle when mixing the rice to avoid breaking the grains.
- Always check that the chicken is fully cooked before serving.
