# Creamy Chicken Pasta

A simple and delicious pasta dish with tender chicken, cherry tomatoes, and a creamy Parmesan sauce. It is easy to prepare, filling, and perfect for a quick lunch or dinner.

**Provided by:** Kevin Claus

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 30 minutes
- Servings: 2

## Ingredients
- 200 g pasta (e.g., penne or fusilli)
- 300 g chicken breast
- 150 g cherry tomatoes
- 1 small onion
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 150 ml cooking cream
- 30 g grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh basil (optional)

## Instructions
- Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to the package instructions until al dente. Before draining, reserve about 100 ml of pasta water.
- Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces. Dice the onion, mince the garlic, and halve the cherry tomatoes.
- Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken, season with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning, and cook for 5–7 minutes until golden and cooked through.
- Add the onion and cook for 2–3 minutes. Stir in the garlic and cherry tomatoes and cook for another 2 minutes.
- Reduce the heat to medium-low. Add the cooking cream and simmer gently for 3–4 minutes.
- Add the drained pasta and Parmesan. Toss until the pasta is coated in the sauce. If the sauce is too thick, add a little reserved pasta water.
- Taste and adjust the seasoning. Garnish with fresh basil and extra Parmesan if desired, then serve immediately.
