Enchilada Lasagne#

Easy lasagne that uses tortilla wraps instead of pasta sheets, layered with a creamy, spicy sauce of ground meat, beans, corn and tomatoes. It’s also easy to make vegetarian. Just swap the ground meat for a vegetarian alternative or an extra can of beans.

Provided by: René
Based on: Mells mexikanische Enchilada-Lasagne on Chefkoch.de

Stats#

  • Cooking Time: 1 hour

  • Servings: 6

Ingredients#

For the sauce#

  • 500 g ground meat or vegetarian alternative

  • 1 red onion

  • 2 garlic cloves

  • 1 red pepper

  • 1 can kidney beans (400 g)

  • 1 can sweet corn (300 g)

  • 1 can diced tomatoes (400 g)

  • 400 g plain yogurt

  • 150 g cream cheese with red pepper and chili

  • Oil for frying

Spices#

  • Sweet paprika, to taste

  • Hot paprika, to taste

  • Chili powder, to taste

  • Tabasco, to taste

  • Salt and pepper, to taste

For layering#

  • 1 pack tortilla wraps (20 cm / 8 inch)

  • 200 g grated cheese

Instructions#

  1. Finely chop the onion and garlic, and dice the red pepper. Drain the kidney beans and corn.

  2. Heat a little oil in a large pan and briefly fry the onion and garlic.

  3. Add the ground meat and brown it well.

  4. Add the red pepper, beans and corn, and fry briefly.

  5. Add the diced tomatoes, yogurt and cream cheese. Stir well and bring to a boil.

  6. Simmer for about 10–15 minutes. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 180 °C.

  7. Season the sauce to taste with paprika, chili powder, tabasco, salt and pepper.

  8. Spread a layer of sauce in a baking dish, then lay tortillas side by side on top (they can overlap slightly, depending on the size of your baking dish).

  9. Keep alternating sauce and tortillas, finishing with a layer of sauce.

  10. Sprinkle the grated cheese on top and bake for about 20 minutes, until the cheese is as brown as you like.

  11. Let it rest for about 5 minutes before cutting, so the layers hold together.

  12. Enjoy!
