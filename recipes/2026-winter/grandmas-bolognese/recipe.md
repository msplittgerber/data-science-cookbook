# Grandma's Bolognese
Bolognese recipe by my grandma to feed me and my siblings fast and easy. Kids did not like chunks of onions, so they will be mashed! My favorite dish ever since.

**Provided by:** Michel

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 1 h (but the longer the better)
- Servings: 4–8

## Ingredients
- 1 kg ground meat
- 2 medium to large onions
- 2 pickle sticks from the jar (plus a quarter of the pickle brine)
- 3 chunks of smoked bacon
- 140 g tomato paste
- 3 dashes of olive oil
- 2 squirts of medium-hot mustard
- 1 slightly heaped tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp bouillon powder
- Water as needed

## Instructions
- Slice onions in half and put them in a large pot with half of the pickle brine and the halved onions.
- Purée the pickle-onion mixture until no chunks are left.
- Fry the meat in small batches in lard over high heat and add it to the large pot.
- Add the tomato paste, olive oil, mustard, pepper and bouillon powder.
- Add water and Stir until mixed. Add the bacon and let it simmer.
- Let the sauce simmer on low heat. The longer, the better!
- Add more water if longer cooking, oil or spices if needed.

## side note
you can change the ground meat to vegan ground meat, That's very delicious, too!
