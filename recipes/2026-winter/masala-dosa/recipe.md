# Masala Dosa

## Stats

- Preparation time: 30 minutes
- Cooking time: 20 minutes
- Servings: 4
- Difficulty: Medium

## Ingredients

### For the dosa batter
- 2 cups rice
- ½ cup urad dal
- ½ teaspoon fenugreek seeds
- Salt to taste

### For the potato filling
- 4 boiled potatoes
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 green chilies
- 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
- Curry leaves
- ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
- 2 tablespoons oil
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Soak rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds for 6 hours.
2. Grind into a smooth batter and ferment overnight.
3. Heat oil in a pan and prepare the potato masala with onions, mustard seeds, curry leaves, turmeric, and potatoes.
4. Heat a dosa pan and spread the batter into a thin circle.
5. Drizzle a little oil around the edges.
6. Place the potato filling in the center.
7. Fold the dosa and serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.

## Hints & Tricks

- Ferment the batter in a warm place for best results.
- Use a well-seasoned cast-iron tawa for crispy dosas.
- Spread the batter quickly while the pan is hot.
- Serve immediately for maximum crispiness.
