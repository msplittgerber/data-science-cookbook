# Menemen – Turkish-Style Scrambled Eggs with Tomatoes and Peppers

Menemen is a traditional Turkish breakfast dish made with eggs, fresh tomatoes, and green peppers, cooked in butter or olive oil. It's one of my absolute favorite breakfast dishes! Simple, delicious, and best enjoyed with fresh, crusty bread. And of course, there's always the big debate in Turkey: with onions or without?

## Stats
- Preparation Time: 10 minutes
- Cooking Time: 15 minutes
- Servings: 2

## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 3 medium tomatoes
- 2 green peppers (preferably Turkish sivri peppers)
- 50 g butter
- 1 medium onion (optional)
- Salt to taste
- Black pepper (optional)
- Red pepper flakes (optional)


## Instructions

1. Finely chop the tomatoes and green peppers. If you prefer menemen with onions, finely chop one small onion as well.
2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
3. If using onions, add them first and cook until softened.
4. Add the green peppers and saute for 2–3 minutes.
5. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook for about 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften and release their juices.
6. Crack the eggs directly into the pan and stir gently until they are fully mixed with the tomatoes and peppers.
7. Season with salt, black pepper, and optional red pepper flakes. Cook until the eggs are set but still soft and creamy.
8. Serve immediately with fresh bread. Enjoy!

**Personal tip:** Don't overcook the eggs! Menemen tastes best when it's still slightly creamy. And don't forget the bread — dipping it into the menemen is half the fun!
