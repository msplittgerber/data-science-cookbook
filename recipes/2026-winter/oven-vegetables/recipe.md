# Oven Vegetables
Oven-roasted vegetables are a simple, colorful dish made by roasting a mix of fresh vegetables until they are tender and lightly caramelized. You can combine all kinds of vegetables, so the recipe is easy to adapt to whatever is in season or already in your kitchen. I love this dish because it is quick to prepare and needs only one baking dish, which means less washing up. 

**Provided by:** Simon Bertelsmeier

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 15 minutes preperation, 30 minutes baking
- Servings: 4

## Ingredients
- 500 g small potatoes
- 2 bell pepper
- 200 g zucchini
- 1 small aubergine
- 2 red onions
- 100g cherry tomatoes
- 1-2 garlic cloves
- 200g carrots
- 200g feta cheese
- 4-5 tbsp olive oil
- pinch of salt
- pinch of pepper
- pinch of herbs
- pinch of paprika powder


## Instructions
- Step 1: Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F)
- Step 2: Wash and chop your vegetables into bite-sized pieces
- Step 3: Place them in a baking dish, drizzle with olive oil, and season with salt, pepper, paprika powder and your favorite herbs
- Step 4: Roast for 25–35 minutes, stirring halfway through, until tender and lightly browned
- Step 5: Serve warm and enjoy!
